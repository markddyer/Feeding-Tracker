# Feeding Tracker

## Overview

**Creative North Star:** a nursery by day, a nightlight by night. The day look is warm, round and a little playful, because this is a joyful time. The night look is the same screen with the light turned down, because it is used at 3am beside a sleeping baby and partner.

**Product context:** a personal iPhone app (and later Apple Watch) for two parents to log a newborn's feeds, kept in step through iCloud, with alarms if no feed is logged. Peers looked at: LilSense, Wee Hours, Mango Baby, littlefeed, Huckleberry. Most baby trackers are either cute and bright (dazzling at 3am) or dark and plain (joyless); this app is both, by time of day.

**Mode per surface:** every screen is an Operate surface: quick taps, facts read at a glance. There is no marketing surface.

**Reference sites:** https://reeoo.com/app-screenshot/lilsense-3am-baby-tracker/, https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6772457183, https://apps.apple.com/app/id1512238725, https://apps.apple.com/app/id1384785720, https://huckleberrycare.com

**Key characteristics:**
- The time since the last feed started is the biggest thing on the home screen, with the window bar under it in the same tile.
- Every fact sits in its own tinted tile, with space between tiles. No sentences where a picture will do. **One written exception: Ask** (AI insights step 3), where a typed question gets an answer in words; even there, the log's facts sit in a "From your log" tile and the NHS's words in their own box.
- Each kind of feed has a colour and a shape, so it reads at night and without colour.
- One big button at the bottom (Start feed, or Finish), reachable by thumb.
- At night nothing is bright, and the letters get thinner.

The approved mockup (day and 3am) is `chosen-look.html`, kept outside the repo in this project's gstack design folder (`designs/design-system-20261115/`). Open it in Safari to see SF Pro Rounded.

## Colors

**Strategy:** full palette, used with care. Warm neutrals carry the screen. One coral main button. Three kind colours (breast, bottle, pumping) and two parent colours carry meaning; they never decorate.

**Light or dark:** set by the use scene, not taste. By day the parents are awake, often in daylight, and the app should feel happy: the light look. At night the room is dark and someone is asleep: the night look. The app switches by itself (see "Day and night" below).

**Rules:**
- Kind colours: pink is breast, blue is bottle, green is pumping. Each has three tokens. `*-tint` fills a tile or row. The plain token (`breast`, `bottle`, `pump`) fills a big area such as the selected side button. `*-mark` draws small shapes, chart bars and lines, because it is deep enough to read on the light tiles (3:1 or better). At night the plain night token does both jobs.
- Parent colours: each parent picks a colour in Settings ("Your colour") from six: `parent-1` (sunflower) and `parent-2` (teal), the defaults, then `lavender`, `plum`, `caramel` and `stone`. They are stored by name in the Member record ("sunflower", "teal", "lavender", "plum", "caramel", "stone"). They mark who did each entry and who is on each night. Use `on-*` for the initial on an avatar. None is red or green, a feed colour or the coral button. Each also has a deeper `*-mark` shade (design 5A, 20 Nov 2026) for the "who does what" dots and bars and the chosen parent in an editor: 3:1 or better on `surface` and `sleep-tint`, every pair told apart, with the initial inside in `on-parent-mark`.
- The window bar (the users' choice B, 21 Nov 2026, `hero-bar-20261121/round2-colours.html`): each part has its own colour, pale while still to come and full once the time has passed it. The wait before the window is misty blue (`wait-tint`, then `wait`), the feeding window coral (`window`, then `primary`), the stretch up to the backup alarm terracotta (`backup-stripe` stripes on `backup-tint`, then `backup`). The misty blue is greyer and paler than the bottle's blue (15 or more apart in colour, both looks). No red and no green: green is pumping, and red would read as a warning about the baby. `track` is left for empty tracks elsewhere (chart grid lines, the watch's bar).
- `notice` is only for problems with the app (sync failed). It is a muted orange, never red, and never says anything about the baby.
- Baths (20 Nov 2026) have a water-blue of their own (`bath`, `bath-mark`, `bath-tint`), told apart from the bottle's blue and the teal parent colour by the colour test.
- Sleep and nappies (17 Nov 2026) use the same three jobs: periwinkle is sleep (`sleep`, `sleep-mark`, `sleep-tint`); yellow is wee (`wee`, `wee-mark`) and brown is poo (`poo`, `poo-mark`), both on one `nappy-tint`. Medicine and growth have no colour of their own: their shapes use `text-muted`.
- No red or green verdicts, ever. Green means pumping and nothing else. The one exception is a poo's colour: `poo-brown`, `poo-yellow`, `poo-green` and `poo-pale` (pale or white, with a thin `text-muted` edge, as it is close to the chip's colour) show the colour a parent picked, as a small swatch, always beside its word ("Green"). They are facts, never a verdict, and never colour anything else.
- Night colours are dimmed copies of the day colours, not new hues, so the screen feels the same.
- Contrast, checked 15 Nov 2026: text on background 12.6:1 (day) and 14.1:1 (night); muted text 4.8:1 or better on every background and tile; coral button text 7.0:1 (day) and 5.2:1 (night); kind marks 3.5:1 or better against every tile. The initial on every parent colour is 4.5:1 or better in both looks. Check again after any colour change.

## Typography

Two typefaces, each with one job:

- **Fredoka** (Google Fonts, SIL Open Font License; bundled as a font file, which is not an SDK) for headings, the baby's name, tile values such as "Left", and button labels. Round and chubby: this is where the joy lives.
- **SF Pro Rounded** (Apple's own, `.fontDesign(.rounded)`) for everything else, and for **every time, duration, amount and count**, always with `.monospacedDigit()`. Fredoka's digits are not all the same width ("11:11" is 70 px wide and "00:00" is 100 px at 40 px), so a ticking timer in Fredoka would wobble. Never set a number in Fredoka.

Sizes (all scale with Dynamic Type, using `relativeTo:`):

| Use | Face | Size and weight (day) | Night weight | Dynamic Type style |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Main time ("2h 05m", "12:04") | SF Pro Rounded | 38 pt bold | medium | `.largeTitle` |
| Screen title, baby's name | Fredoka | 26 pt semibold | regular | `.title` |
| Big button label | Fredoka | 20 pt semibold | regular | `.title3` |
| Tile value ("Left") | Fredoka | 19 pt semibold | regular | `.title3` |
| Day heading in History | Fredoka | 18 pt semibold | regular | `.headline` |
| Totals ("8", "240") | SF Pro Rounded | 22 pt bold | medium | `.title2` |
| Body, bar labels | SF Pro Rounded | 15-17 pt regular | regular | `.body`, `.subheadline` |
| Chips ("12m", "30 ml") | SF Pro Rounded | 12.5 pt heavy | semibold | `.caption` |
| Tile labels ("NEXT SIDE") | SF Pro Rounded | 11 pt heavy, upper case, +0.07 em | semibold | `.caption2` |

At night every weight drops one or two steps (see the table), so fewer pixels light up in a dark room.

## Layout

- One column on the phone and on the folded Duo (382 and 385 pt). A 16 pt gutter. Tiles are 10-12 pt apart.
- **Four tabs**: Home, History and Graphs (the user's choice, 16 Nov 2026), and Journal (the milestones journal, `07-milestones-journal.md`), in the system tab bar (on the folded Duo, iOS puts it in the bar down the right-hand side). The chosen tab is in `text`, not coral. Settings is the gear at the top of Home.
- **Home is calm** (the users' choice B, 24 Nov 2026, `docs/planning/13-calm-home.md`): people switch off when a screen is busy, so Home shows two boxes, not eight. The baby's name and age sit in the top bar beside Ask and Settings (no greeting). Then, top to bottom: an ask card if one is waiting, the "Last night" line (each morning until 12:00), the hero tile (the main time, the window bar, and under a thin line the last feed's chips and "Next ◐ Left", with who did the last feed beside "Last feed"), one plain `surface` box with a row each for Sleep, Nappy, each medicine and Bath (shape, a bold line, a muted line, and the buttons on the right, or Wee, Poo and Both under the nappy's words), then three folded headings, Tonight, Today and Recent. Start feed (two thirds of the width) and Pumping (the other third) are pinned to the bottom in one row; the rest scrolls behind them. At the accessibility text sizes they stack.
- **Folded headings:** a thin `surface` line, the name in Fredoka, a one-line summary in muted text on the right ("7 feeds · 210 ml · 90 ml pumped", "Wee and poo, 14:58"), and a chevron. They start closed, and each phone remembers what is open. Tonight's who-is-on line, its buttons, any ask line and any "waiting for the other phone" line always show under its heading; only the week of nights folds. Today holds the 24-hour strip and totals; Recent the last 24 hours' entries as History rows in one `surface` box, without their own tints.
- During a feed the bottom holds Left / Right / Bottle (three equal buttons) and, below them, "Stop · discard" (150 pt wide) beside a wider Finish.
- **Unfolded Duo, the Book** (the users' choice A of `home-calm-20261124/round3-duo-bold.html`, 24 Nov 2026): the left page is "now": the hero tile, then sleep, nappy, Tonight, each medicine and bath as plain cards two to a row, every card the same size (`EvenGrid`: each as tall as the tallest, the same gaps everywhere; the users' ask, 24 Nov 2026), with no log buttons, and Start feed and Pumping at the bottom left, away from the fold. A card has its shape and title on one line and up to two lines under them, across its whole width. Tonight's card shows who is on (their circle) and any ask or wait; a tap opens a window with tonight's line, its buttons and the week of nights, as the page has no room to show them in place without scrolling. Tonight comes before the medicines and Bath, and several medicines share one "Medicines" card, so there are never more than three rows. The left page never scrolls (the users' rule, 24 Nov 2026): the cards shrink together to fit, down to a title and one line each, and a short card shows its most important line (Tonight's wait or ask before who is on; a poo's colour and texture before the time). Only at the largest text sizes, when even the shortest cards can't fit, does it scroll, rather than cut words off. What waits above the top tile on the phone (a sync problem, an ask from the other parent, "Last night" or the AI card's line) sits at the top of the right page instead. The right page is "today": those lines and the totals line, which stay put, then the last 24 hours as a timeline that scrolls, earlier at the top like a calendar (the users' ask, 24 Nov 2026) and opening at its end, at now: an hour ruler, the night shaded, sleeps as lilac bars and feeds as pink or blue blocks as long as they lasted, and beside them each entry as one `surface` pill, as wide as it needs, holding its time, its shapes and numbers (plain inside, never a pill in a pill) and the circle of who did it (the users' ask, 24 Nov 2026). Under "now", the feeding window and the backup alarm still to come, as narrow marks with their words. Where entries crowd, the page stretches so each label stays beside its time. A tap on an entry or a sleep's bar opens it. Along the bottom of the right page, under the right thumb, one row of log buttons, every one the same width and height (the users' ask, 24 Nov 2026), with a long word on two lines or a little smaller rather than cut off: Woke up (or Fell asleep), Wee, Poo, Both, the medicine (a menu when there are several) and Bath. The phones' last sync shows under the timeline. Nothing crosses the fold. Closed, in laptop pose or in Split View, Home is the phone page.
- Leading alignment everywhere. Nothing is centred except labels inside buttons.

## Elevation & Depth

Flat. No shadows, glows, gradients or blur. Depth comes from tint: background, then tile, then chip (which uses the background colour again, so it reads as a hole in the tile). Never a tile inside a tile.

**One exception, the parents' own ask** (design D7 and 5B, 20 Nov 2026): the Undo card floats over the screen in the system's Liquid Glass (`.glassEffect`), the only blurred surface in the app. Plain system glass: iOS's Reduce Transparency applies. At night it stays dim (no bright refraction over the dark screen) and its text meets 4.5:1. Don't add glass anywhere else without asking.

## Shapes

- Radii: chips 9 pt; small buttons 14 pt; tiles and big buttons 20 pt; the hero tile 26 pt; pills and avatars fully round. An inner corner is the outer corner minus the gap.
- **A shape for each kind of feed:**
  - Breast: a half-moon (`circle.lefthalf.filled` / `circle.righthalf.filled`). The filled half is the side.
  - Bottle: a tall baby bottle, drawn as a SwiftUI `Shape`.
  - Pumping: two drops, drawn as a SwiftUI `Shape`.
  - Sleep: a crescent moon. Wee: one drop. Poo: a soft swirl. Both: the drop and the swirl side by side. Medicine: a spoon. Growth: a line going up. On the Lock Screen's Sleep and nappy widget, Fell asleep is a closed eye and Woke up an open eye (the users' ask, 17 Nov 2026: the words didn't fit). All are SwiftUI `Shape`s in `Shared/FeedShapes.swift`.
  - The shape appears on every chip, row, button and chart key for that kind. A chip shows the shape and the number ("◐ 12m", "bottle 30 ml"), so letters such as "L" and "R" are not needed.
- No ring on the phone (the users' choice C, 21 Nov 2026): the window bar shows how far through the window the time is. During a feed, the side being fed (its half-moon) or the bottle sits at the right of the timer, 40 pt, in its `*-mark` colour.

## Components

- **Big button** (Start feed, Finish): at least 60 pt tall, full width, `primary`, Fredoka label with an icon. Pressed: scales to 0.97 with a medium haptic. Disabled: 40% opacity.
- **Start feed asks when** (the user's choice, 24 Nov 2026): a tap opens the system menu, up from the button, with Now nearest the thumb, then Just finished (started about 10, 20, 30 or 45 min ago) and Earlier (the form). On a bottle-only phone the milks (and "Breastfeed (Sam)") are listed under "Now", so a bottle still starts in two taps. A tap outside starts nothing. The Lock Screen, Control Center, Siri and the watch still start a feed at once.
- **Soft big button** ("Stop · discard", Pumping): `surface` background. "Stop · discard" always asks "Remove this feed?" before removing anything. Pumping, a third of the width, has its shape above the word.
- **Side buttons** (Left, Right, Bottle): 64 pt tall, shape above the label. The current side fills with the breast colour.
- **Tile:** tinted by what it shows (a kind's tint, or plain surface). A small upper-case label, then the value. Home itself has no tinted tiles since 24 Nov 2026: colour there is on the shapes, the window bar, and the hero during a feed.
- **Care row** (Home's list): the kind's shape in a 28 pt column, a bold line and a muted line, and its round button (`PillButton`) on the right; under the words when they don't fit. 64 pt tall at least. Rows are parted by a 1.5 pt line in the page colour. Touch and hold Woke up for an earlier time; touch and hold a medicine to change the list.
- **Chip:** `background` colour on a tile, shape plus number. Never a sentence. Where chips or round buttons sit straight on the page (the Duo's timeline, Home's Tonight), they use `surface` instead.
- **Outside the app** (added 16 Nov 2026): the Lock Screen widget and the control are drawn by iOS in one tint, so they have no colours of their own, except the control's `primary` tint when on. SF Pro Rounded with fixed-width digits, the same "1h 40m" as Home ("1:40" in the small circle), the same facts-only words, and the SF Symbols `waterbottle` (no feed running) and `stopwatch` (a feed running). The wide Lock Screen widget puts the time on its own first line ("1 hour, 40 minutes", or "12 minutes" while a feed runs) and the words under it ("Last feed · window 05:00", or "Feed running, from 04:28"), then its buttons: "Last feed 1h 40m ago" on one line was cut off on the iPhone Duo, and iOS sets the widget's width. Its time is iOS's own self-updating format, so it reads "1 hour, 40 minutes" rather than Home's "1h 40m": with a prepared moment for every minute, the widget's saved file grew too big on the Duo and iOS showed a grey stand-in (the user's choices, 16 Nov 2026). A Control Center button set to one milk shows a letter instead, because a small button shows only its icon: `f.circle.fill` for Formula and `e.circle.fill` for Expressed milk (the user's choice, 16 Nov 2026).
- **Apple Watch** (added 16 Nov 2026, the user's choices from side-by-side mockups):
  - **A black background by day as well as at night.** This departs from the phone's cream day background on purpose: Apple's advice for watches, so the screen's edge disappears into the watch and nothing is bright when a wrist is lifted. By day the watch uses `#2A1F18` for buttons and tiles, `#FFF4E2` for text, `#BFA995` for muted text, `#3A2C22` for the empty bar and `#7A4A35` for the window, with the phone's day coral and feed colours. At night it uses the phone's night colours on black, and every weight drops as on the phone. Contrast, checked 16 Nov 2026: text on black 19.3:1 (day) and 14.8:1 (night); muted text on a button 7.1:1 and 5.0:1; coral button text 7.0:1 and 5.2:1; the selected side's label 6.0:1 and 3.4:1 (the phone's night side button, large text).
  - **Three pages, each one screen** (the parents' choices of 21 Nov 2026, `11-watch-parity.md`, `designs/watch-parity-20261121/round5-square.html`): every main page fits with no scrolling and no unused space on 42 mm and 49 mm. The small label ("LAST FEED", "YOUR FEED", "AWAKE", "TODAY") sits beside the clock (a `topBarLeading` toolbar item with an inline title, so no room is kept for a large one and the page starts just under it; it works on watchOS 27). ## Day and night

- **Automatic** (the default): the night look runs during the household's night hours (the same hours as the night rota: 21:00 to 07:00 by default) and the day look the rest of the time.
- In Settings, each phone can choose **Automatic**, **Always day** (added 16 Nov 2026, the user's choice), **Always night**, or **Match iPhone** (follows the phone's own Dark Mode).
- The app sets `.preferredColorScheme` to match the look, so sheets, pickers and the keyboard follow it and nothing flashes white at night.
- The change is a 0.3 second cross-fade, never a flash.

## Do's and Don'ts

- Do: put every time, duration and amount in SF Pro Rounded with fixed-width digits.
- Do: give each kind of feed its shape as well as its colour, everywhere it appears.
- Do: show facts as pictures first (bar, chart, shapes, avatars) and keep words to labels.
- Do: check contrast and Dynamic Type (up to the largest accessibility size) on every screen, in both looks.
- Do: keep the night look dim: no white, no bright fills, thinner letters, fades only.
- Don't: use "due", "missed", "overdue" or "should", or show targets, scores or red/green verdicts.
- Don't: add a third typeface, or set a number in Fredoka.
- Don't: use shadows, glows, gradients or a tile inside a tile.
- Don't: use a photo or real name of the family anywhere in the code or pictures; the code is public.
- Do (the guarded rule, R1, 20 Nov 2026, in place of "never a comparison"): show each parent's part side by side as facts (counts, time, nights) in the parent colours, the same on both phones.
- Don't: name a winner, use a score word ("more", "fair", "behind"), or colour a parent's part red or green.

## Motion

- **Approach:** gentle by day, still at night.
- **Easing:** enter ease-out, exit ease-out, move ease-in-out.
- **Duration:** micro 80 ms (button press), short 200 ms (chips appearing), medium 300 ms (bar changes, the day/night cross-fade).
- **Night:** fades only, no movement, haptics in place of animation. Respect Reduce Motion at all times. One exception the parents accepted (5B, 20 Nov 2026): the Undo card's 10-second line moves at night too.
- **The one authored moment:** when a feed is logged, a soft haptic, and the bar starts again from empty. At night, the haptic only.
