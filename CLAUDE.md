## Design System
Read DESIGN.md before visual or UI work: it defines the fonts, colours, spacing and look. Ask the user before departing from it. When reviewing or testing UI, flag code that doesn't match DESIGN.md.

## Hard rules
- **The repo is public. Never commit personal data.** That means names, birth dates, feed logs, screenshots with real data, device UDIDs or Apple IDs. Personal context goes in `CLAUDE.local.md`, which is gitignored. Docs refer to "Parent A", "Parent B" and "the baby".
- **No third-party SDKs** for analytics, crash reporting, ads or tracking. All data stays in the household's iCloud, with one exception (AI insights, D2 and D12 of `docs/planning/12-ai-insights-gauntlet.md`): the facts sheet (`InsightFacts`, numbers with no text and no medicines) and a typed question may go to Anthropic, only after both parents have said yes, each on their own phone. Anthropic's Claude for Foundation Models Swift package is the one outside library allowed, for that and nothing else.
- **Feed timing is measured from the start of a feed.** Never store a "next due" time. Alerts come from `reconcile()` in `FeedingCore`, which is a pure function. `reconcile` reads only the unencrypted top-level fields.
- **When unsure, alert too much rather than too little.** Any change that could make a phone alert later, or not at all, must follow the acknowledgement rule in the plan.
- **The CloudKit Production schema is permanent.** Change the schema only by editing `schema.ckdb`, and only by adding to it. Never deploy to Production without the user's go-ahead. Never delete a CloudKit zone automatically.
- **AlarmKit has no entitlement.** It needs only `NSAlarmKitUsageDescription`. Don't add a made-up entitlement, because that breaks signing.
- **No verdicts, triage or doses about the baby**, from the app or the AI (D5, relaxed on 22 Nov 2026; Anthropic's Usage Policy): never say this baby is fine, normal or unwell; never set the baby's numbers against targets ("due", "missed", "overdue" for a feed), thresholds or other babies; never rank causes or say how urgent something is; never give, time or check a medicine dose; no red or green verdicts. Plain words, "should" included, are fine for general information, and NHS passages keep their own words. Everything the AI writes is marked as AI. Keep the disclaimer. The app's intended purpose is written in `docs/planning/12-ai-insights-gauntlet.md` ("Intended purpose"): check new screens against it.
- Spikes and testing use the **Feeding Dev** variant, which has its own bundle ID, container and App Group. Never point development builds at the production container.

## Layout
- `FeedingCore/`: a Swift package that depends only on Foundation. It holds the domain model, `reconcile` (an alert a change moves into the past before it went off goes off at once: `passedAlerts`, `catchUpAlerts`, 21 Nov 2026), the rota and night logic, the feed and pump details with versioned payload encoding (`PayloadCoder`), the field-level merge (`merge(local:server:changedLocally:)`), the next-side suggestion, the 24-hour summary and duplicate detection. It must build and pass `swift test` on Linux as well as macOS. Adding a payload field that matters means raising that payload's `currentVersion` (see `VersionedPayload`). The household file is also read by older builds: what a newer build wrote and an older one doesn't know (a field name, a record kind, an entry, a part of the file) is skipped and kept (`KeptJSON`, step 0 of the journal), and while a phone holds no events its alarms stay as they are. Raise `HouseholdData.currentFormat` only for a change an older build must not read at all. The journal's firsts (`docs/planning/07-milestones-journal.md`) are events of kind `milestone`: their details, limits and save rules are in `Store/Milestones.swift` (`EventPayload` version 3, or 4 once a photo is moved in its square (`MediaCrop`, the `crops` field); the removed-photos list joins on a merge instead of the later change winning), and the baby's age and the Firsts list in `Insights/Journal.swift`. The rules for their photos and videos (the allowance tally, the space reserve, feeds first, the clean-up, the share's members, the new-first notice) are in `Store/MediaRules.swift`; a first's delete or a photo taken out is held for Undo (`deleteMilestone`, `undoHeld`, `HouseholdData.mayUpload`).
- `FeedingCore/Sources/FeedingInsights/`: AI insights steps 1 to 3 (`docs/planning/12-ai-insights-gauntlet.md`), a second library in the same package. It uses `FeedingCore`; `FeedingCore` can never use it (R1), so nothing the alerts read can depend on it. Foundation only, tested on Linux (`FeedingInsightsTests`). `InsightFacts.swift`: the numbers the AI may see (yesterday, last night, 28 days and nights, weights), with no text and no medicines. `InsightSafety.swift`: the word reader, the danger-sign check that runs before any AI (`DangerSigns`), medicine questions answered on the phone (`MedicineQuestions`), and `AnswerCheck`, the only way to make a `CheckedCard` (the AI fills a fixed form; code writes every number and checks each pattern against the facts). `InsightCards.swift` (step 2): when a card is made, what is sent (the log as JSON with no spaces or line breaks, about 17,500 tokens a card; "What the AI sees" shows it spaced out, `readablePrompt`), the form, and storing the card. `InsightQuestions.swift` (step 3, Ask): every rule for a typed question: the route (danger signs on the words and with the question before them, medicines (the logged time only for a look back), the gate, then the AI), what is sent (the last 7 days and nights, today so far (`InsightFacts.todaySoFar`), the question before it within 10 minutes), the form (the help flag first, up to 2 AI sentences), the repeat rule and the 24-hour expiry; Ask's checked answer (`CheckedAnswer`, `AnswerCheck.sentenceProblem`) is in `InsightSafety.swift`. `NHSPassages.swift`: the NHS passages word for word with "Information from the NHS website, as at" their copy date, the chooser (by kinds logged and age, never by a value), and Parent A's ticks (`passageChecks`, one Setting record per passage or summary, `passageCheck-<id>`); a passage or summary is offered only once ticked, within 90 days.
- `App/Insights/`: Get help now (`GetHelpNowView`, the NHS's urgent list with Call 999 and Call 111, opened from Settings › About) and Settings › NHS information (`PassagesListView`, `PassageCheckView`, where the ticks are made). Step 2: `InsightEngine.swift` (the call to Claude Sonnet 5.5 through Anthropic's package, App Attest on phones and the development key only in the simulator, the tries and the gate before every call), `InsightCardViews.swift` (what a screen shows, `CardShown`; the card sheet, the AI line, the AI mark, "This was wrong") and `InsightSettingsView.swift` (Settings › AI insights: each parent's yes, the off switch, "What the AI sees", the pause). Home's "Last night" line with its card is `App/Home/LastNightLine.swift`. `App/Spikes/AIConnectionCheck.swift` (Little Log Dev only) checks a live call from a real phone with a made-up week. Step 3: `QuestionsView.swift` (Ask, a sheet from Home's top bar), `QuestionLog.swift` (Ask's questions on this phone: 24 hours, complete file protection, no backup, never synced) and `InsightEngine.ask`/`askAI`.
- `project.yml`: the XcodeGen spec for the two variants, `Feeding` and `FeedingDev`. Generate the project with `xcodegen generate` and never hand-edit the generated `Feeding.xcodeproj`. Info.plists and entitlements are generated into `Generated/` (gitignored). The Apple team ID goes in `Config/Local.xcconfig` (gitignored; see the `.example`).
- `schema.ckdb`: the CloudKit schema as text, loaded with `xcrun cktool import-schema`. Validated by `cktool` and imported into the **Dev** container's Development environment on 14 Nov 2026 (the export matched the file). On 18 Nov 2026 the journal's `Media` and `MediaTally` types were added (for the photos zone, built in `RecordMapping`) and imported into Dev the same way; the file also holds `cloudkit.share`, which CloudKit made by itself when the household was first shared. The export matched the file again. Deployed to Production on 19 Nov 2026 with the parents' go-ahead (`docs/planning/08-going-live.md`, G1); Production's export matched the file. On 21 Nov 2026 the four "who does what" `Event` fields (`doneBy`, `wakeID`, `gotUpBy`, `gotUpFor`) were added to Production the same way, with the parents' go-ahead (ET2 in `09-who-does-what.md`), and Production's export matched the file again. From now on, only add to it.
- `App/`: the iPhone app.
- `Watch/`: the Apple Watch app (`Watch/App`), its watch face pieces (`Watch/Widgets`) and the file they share (`Watch/Shared`), one per variant (`FeedingWatch`, `FeedingDevWatch`; bundle ID `com.tracker.feeding.dev.watchkitapp` for Dev). It talks only to its own iPhone over WatchConnectivity: the phone sends a `WatchState`, and the watch's taps come back as `WatchRequest`s that `WatchRelay` (in `App/Model`) applies with `applyWatch` (in `FeedingCore`). The watch picks the IDs of what it creates, so a tap sent twice changes nothing. It builds into the iPhone app, and installs on the paired watch with it.
- `Widgets/`: the widget extension (the Lock Screen widgets, Last feed and Sleep and nappy, and the controls: "Start or finish a feed", Sleep, Nappy and Medicine), one per variant (`FeedingWidgets`, `FeedingDevWidgets`). It only reads `household.json` in the App Group.
- `App/Sync/MediaSync.swift`, `App/Media/MediaFiles.swift` and `App/Media/MediaStore.swift`: the journal's photos and videos in iCloud and on the phone (`MediaStore` makes the journal's copy: scaled, JPEG or MP4, no place in it) (task T6 of `07-milestones-journal.md`), in a `Photos` zone of their own that no sync engine fetches, moved one at a time behind the feeds. Feeding Dev only (`AppConfig.photosEnabled`) until the `Media` types are in the Production schema. Its log lines start "media:".
- `Shared/`: code built into both the app and the widget extension: the start and finish actions (`FeedIntents.swift`, which run in the app's process: `allowedExecutionTargets` `[.main]`), the sleep, nappy and medicine actions (`CareIntents.swift`), the widgets' views (`LastFeedWidgetView`, `CareWidgetView`) and their words (`GlanceText`). `FeedShapes.swift` holds every kind's shape (bottle, drops, moon, drop, swirl, spoon, growth line) and is also built into the watch targets. Code that runs only in the app goes in `#if !FEEDING_WIDGETS` or in `App/`.
- `App/Graphs/`: the Graphs tab (Swift Charts), its words (`GraphText`); the per-day facts are `graphDays` in `FeedingCore/Sources/FeedingCore/Insights/Graphs.swift`. The daily rhythm report and the weight graph are in `RhythmTiles.swift`; their facts are `rhythmDays` in `FeedingCore/Sources/FeedingCore/Insights/Rhythm.swift`. Each graph opens its own page (`GraphPage.swift`; the charts, shared by tiles and pages, in `GraphCharts.swift`); the entries behind a day are `graphDayDetails`, `RhythmDay.entries` and `weights` in `FeedingCore/Sources/FeedingCore/Insights/GraphDetails.swift`, built from the same rules as the bars.
- Sleep, nappies, growth and medicine (`06-more-than-feeds.md`): each is an `Event` with its own kind and its details in the encrypted payload (no schema change). The logging rules are in `FeedingCore/Sources/FeedingCore/Store/MoreThanFeeds.swift` (Start feed ends a running sleep; Stop · discard puts it back; the medicine list). Home's care list (the phone's rows, the Duo's facts and its row of log buttons), the poo panel and Change time are in `App/Home/CareTiles.swift`; Home's folded parts, and the open Duo's Tonight card and window, in `App/Home/HomeFolds.swift`; what waits above the top tile (`HomeTopLines`, at the top of the open Duo's right page) in `App/Home/HomeView.swift`; the Duo's right page and its timeline in `App/Home/DayColumn.swift`, the words in `CareText.swift`, History's rows in `FeedRows.swift` (`CareRow`), and the editor in `CareEditor.swift`. The tabs (Home, History, Graphs) are in `App/ContentView.swift`.
- Who does what (`docs/planning/09-who-does-what.md`, engineering review 20 Nov 2026): "done by", the wake-up ID and "got up" are four encrypted `Event` record fields (`doneBy`, `wakeID`, `gotUpBy`, `gotUpFor`), written only with a value; their stamps are in the payload, set only with a value, so a stamp with no value means an older build dropped it, and the app fetches that entry again by name (`entriesMissingWhoValues`, `HouseholdSync.fetchMissingWhoValues`). Every sleep ending carries its wake-up ID (`keepSleepEnding`, `MoreThanFeeds.swift`). `WhoDidIt` (`Insights/Shares.swift`) is the one answer sheet every screen reads: who did each entry ("saved" or "worked out"), who got up, copies logged on both phones (`duplicateCareEntries` for sleeps, nappies, medicines and baths), the page's facts, each number's list and "Last night". `EventKind.countsAsWork` is the one list of kinds that count. "Who breastfeeds?" is the `breastfeeder` setting.
- App icons: `App/Assets.xcassets` (phone) and `Watch/App/Assets.xcassets` (watch), `AppIcon` for Feeding and `AppIcon-Dev` for Feeding Dev. Never edit the PNGs by hand: the picture is `tools/app-icon.svg`, and `swift tools/make-app-icons.swift tools/app-icon.svg` makes all four from it (see "App icon" in `DESIGN.md`).
- `App/Design/`: `DESIGN.md` in code. Colours with day and night values (`Theme`), the day/night switch (`LookModifier`), text styles (`.feedText(...)`), the feed shapes and the small parts (Tile, Chip, Avatar). `App/Fonts/` holds Fredoka and its licence.

- Watch app in the watch simulator, with made-up states (Feeding Dev only): build with `xcodebuild build -project Feeding.xcodeproj -scheme FeedingDevWatch -destination 'generic/platform=watchOS Simulator' CODE_SIGNING_ALLOWED=NO`, install with `xcrun simctl install <watch> <path>/Debug-watchsimulator/FeedingDevWatch.app`, and launch with `-sampleWatch` and `idle`, `running`, `other`, `bottleOnly`, `bottleRunning`, `amount`, `faceStart` (the watch face's Start feed, asking "Formula or expressed?"), `waiting`, `firstFeed`, `notSetUp`, `asleep` or `awake` (the last two open on page two, Sleep and nappy), or `breastfeedRunning` ("Breastfeed (Sam)" running on a bottle-only watch); add `-sampleBreastfeeder YES` for that choice on a bottle-only watch; add `-sampleLook night` for night, and `-watchPage 2` (or `3`, Today; with `-sampleMedicines YES` for three medicines, which share a "Medicine ›" row) to open any state on that page; `-sampleFeedAgo 185` puts the last feed that many minutes ago; `-watchScreen logged` (or `pooDetails`, `pooConsistency` for its second step, `pump`, `medicines`, and step 2's `addToFeed`, `justFinished`, `earlierFeed`, `bottle`, and step 3's `tonight`, `woke`) shows that screen; `-sampleTonight` `me`, `other` or `asked` sets tonight's rota (nobody on otherwise); `-watchFaceGallery YES` the big watch face piece at four moments. Use the 42 mm Series 10 to check that a screen fits.
- The phone-to-watch link, in simulators: pair a watch simulator with the iPhone 18 Pro one (`xcrun simctl pair <watch> <phone>`), build FeedingDev signed for the phone simulator (the watch app comes in `Debug-watchsimulator`), install both, launch the phone with the sample-data flags above, then launch the watch app with no `-sampleWatch`. `-watchSendTest start` (or `finish`, `sleep`, `nappy`, `bath`, `pooDetails` for a poo then its details, `justFinished` for a past feed then a side and a 40 ml bottle) sends that tap from the watch two seconds after the link is up, as nothing can tap the watch simulator by command. `-watchFaceTapTest YES` does what a tap on the watch face's Start feed does (`finish`: its Finish, on the feed running then; `sleep` and `nappy`: the Sleep and Nappy pieces), four seconds after launch (`simctl openurl` can't open a link on the watch simulator).
- Sync log from a real phone (Feeding Dev only; record names and errors, no feed details): `xcrun devicectl device copy from --device <device id> --domain-type appGroupDataContainer --domain-identifier group.com.tracker.feeding.dev --source Library/Caches/sync-log.txt --destination sync-log.txt`. Only `Library/` in the App Group can be copied this way; `household.json` at its top can't.
- Real watch: Xcode 27 pairs a watch with the Mac directly, not through its phone. In Device Hub (`/Applications/Xcode.app/Contents/Applications/DeviceHub.app`), choose **+** or File, then **Pair Nearby Device…**, then **Apple Watch**, and leave that window open; on the unlocked watch, Settings → Privacy & Security → Developer Mode then appears, with "Pair with <Mac>" and a six-digit code. Until a watch is registered in the developer account, the phone build can't be signed, as the watch app is inside it.

The phone must be unlocked. A phone that is new to the developer account also needs `-allowProvisioningDeviceRegistration`, or the build fails with "isn't registered in your developer account".

## Testing
- Unit tests use Swift Testing, in `FeedingCore`. They must cover these time edge cases: clock changes in both directions, 30-minute time zone offsets, travel, the clock being set back, midnight, and the 21:00 night boundary.
- Simulators include the iPhone Duo, which needs its own simulator runtime (`xcodebuild -downloadPlatform iOS -buildVersion 24B91016s`). `simctl` can't fold it; folding needs the pose buttons in Device Hub. Most app extensions can't run in the Duo simulator, so test widgets in an ordinary iPhone simulator. AlarmKit needs the user's real devices. The device checklist is in the plan.
